ABOUT US

Ocean Beds is part of Ocean Holidays Group, the UK's number 1 Florida receptive tour operator, and the UK's largest growing travel group in the UK in 2022 compared to pre-pandemic levels. Launched in 2013, Ocean Beds aims to give the highest levels of service and support to our partners, and their clients while on holiday from our Welcome Center, which is based in Kissimmee and managed by our own Ocean Holidays Team.

Ocean Beds specialises in delivering an unrivalled Florida Homes programme as well as a vast selection of hotels, including budget to resort-style accommodation, whilst providing wholesale rates at handpicked hotels across Florida and the rest of the USA. Ocean Beds offers the latest technology with a fast XML and in-house extranet capability for effortless distribution.

For more information please contact one of our Business Development Team below. We can offer full support to partners in technology, sales support, training as well as day-to-day contact with a dedicated Account Manager.
